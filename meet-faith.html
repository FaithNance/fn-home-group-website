Her Story

Making Real Estate Feel Exciting, Not Overwhelming

Faith Nance is a REALTOR® and founder of FN Home Group, proudly serving Southern Middle Tennessee. She believes buying or selling a home should feel exciting — not overwhelming — so she focuses on making every step simple, honest, and personalized.

Whether a client is purchasing their first home, upgrading, downsizing, relocating, exploring luxury real estate, or simply trying to understand their options, Faith is committed to providing knowledgeable guidance and an elevated experience from start to finish.

Her Philosophy

More Than an Agent

Faith wants to be more than someone who helps you buy or sell a house. She aims to be a lasting resource — offering educational information, local recommendations, trusted vendor connections, homeowner resources, and continued support before, during, and after your transaction.

She wants every client to feel supported without ever feeling judged, embarrassed, pressured, or confused. That means answering every question honestly, explaining things in plain language, and never pushing a client toward a decision that doesn't feel right for them.

Before Real Estate

A Background Built on Hard Work

Before transitioning into real estate full-time, Faith spent approximately ten years working for General Motors. That experience taught her the importance of hard work, clear communication, consistency, and advocating for the people who trust her — values she carries into every client relationship today.

Outside of Real Estate

Life in Southern Middle Tennessee

Outside of work, Faith enjoys spending time with her husband and their two daughters, exploring local communities, visiting the beach, and enjoying everything Southern Middle Tennessee has to offer. That local, hands-on connection to the area is part of what shapes the guidance she offers clients every day.
