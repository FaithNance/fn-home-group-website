Research Tools

School & Utility Research Links

Faith encourages every relocating buyer to independently research schools, utilities, and local services for any specific address they're considering. Here are a few helpful starting points.

School Research

Contact the relevant county school district directly, or review the Tennessee Department of Education's school directory for zoning and enrollment information.

Utilities

Utility providers vary by community. Local city or county government websites typically list electric, water, and gas providers for a given address.

Local Government

City and county government websites are a reliable source for permitting, zoning, tax, and public service information.

Please verify independently. School zoning, utility providers, and local services can vary by exact address and may change over time. Faith recommends confirming these details directly with the relevant school district, utility provider, or government office before making a decision.
A General Moving Timeline

Planning Your Relocation

  • Start researching communities and general market conditions early, even before you're ready to buy.
  • Connect with a lender to understand financing options, including any out-of-state considerations.
  • Schedule a virtual consultation with Faith to talk through your goals and timeline.
  • Begin a remote home search with Faith's guidance, including virtual tours where available.
  • Plan a visit to tour top contender homes in person, if possible, before writing an offer.
  • Coordinate your move, including vendor and service connections in your new area.
