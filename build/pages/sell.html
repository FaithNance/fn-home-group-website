The Selling Process

What to Expect, Step by Step

Initial Consultation

Faith starts by learning about your home, your timeline, and your goals so she can tailor her approach to what matters most to you.

Property Evaluation

Faith walks through your home to understand its condition, features, and any updates that may affect pricing or marketing.

Comparative Market Analysis

Using recently sold, pending, and active comparable listings, Faith prepares a market analysis to help guide pricing strategy.

Preparing the Property

Faith offers honest, practical guidance on what preparation (if any) may help your home show its best — without pressuring you into unnecessary projects.

Pricing Strategy

Together, you'll land on a pricing strategy that reflects the market, your timeline, and your goals.

Professional Marketing

Faith creates a personalized marketing plan for your home, which may include professional photography, online listing exposure, and targeted promotion.

Showings

Faith coordinates showings and keeps you informed of feedback and activity throughout the listing period.

Offer Review

When offers come in, Faith reviews each one with you in detail, explaining terms, contingencies, and what they mean for your bottom line and timeline.

Inspections & Appraisal

Once under contract, the buyer will typically schedule an inspection, and an appraisal will usually follow if the buyer is financing the purchase.

Negotiations

Faith advocates for you through any negotiations that arise from inspection findings or appraisal results.

Closing Preparation

Faith helps you understand and prepare for closing, coordinating with the title company, lender, and other parties involved.

Faith's Approach

Personalized Marketing, Not a One-Size-Fits-All Plan

Every home and every seller's goals are different, so Faith builds a marketing plan around what makes your property and your situation unique — rather than applying the exact same playbook to every listing.

  • Thoughtful, honest pricing conversations grounded in market data
  • Professional photography and listing presentation
  • Clear, proactive communication throughout the listing period
  • Guidance through every offer, negotiation, and milestone
A Possible Resource for Sellers

Epique Edge

Epique Edge is a financing program offered through a third-party provider, made available to Epique Realty clients, that may help qualifying sellers cover eligible expenses like paint, flooring, cleaning, staging, and minor repairs before listing — without a mortgage or lien on the property. It's one possible option among several — not a required service, and not every seller or property is guaranteed to qualify.

See the full breakdown of how it works, possible uses, and important financial details, or take a quick self-assessment to think through what your home may need before it lists.
