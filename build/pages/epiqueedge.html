What Is Epique Edge?

A Flexible Way to Help Prepare Your Home for Sale

Epique Edge is a financing program offered through a third party provider, made available to Epique Realty clients. It's designed to give qualifying sellers more flexibility when preparing a home for market, without requiring cash up front. Here's what the program may offer to qualifying homeowners:

  • Access to an unsecured line of credit of up to $50,000
  • No mortgage or lien placed on the property
  • A soft credit inquiry during the initial eligibility process
  • No interest charged on unused funds
  • Interest charged only on funds actually used
  • Flexibility to work with the homeowner's chosen contractor or vendor, subject to program terms
  • Repayment of used funds at closing or under the applicable loan terms
  • Mobile friendly access to account information and updates
  • An application process that may provide an initial result quickly

Eligibility, credit limits, rates, fees, and terms are determined by the program provider and are subject to approval. Program availability and details may change.

The Process

How It Works

Review the available credit limit, rate, fees, repayment terms, and all required agreements directly with the financing provider.

Transparency Matters

Important Financial Details

  • A one time program fee may apply, with published provider materials referencing a fee of up to $499.
  • Interest is charged only on funds actually used, not on your total available credit line.
  • Provider materials reference annual percentage rates that may range from approximately 8.99% to 16.99%, subject to approval.
  • Qualification may consider factors such as credit history, available home equity, and the property's list price.
  • Provider materials reference a minimum FICO score of approximately 680 for eligibility.
  • Funds used may be due at the sale of the property or after approximately 12 months, depending on the final signed agreement.
Rates, fees, credit requirements, available amounts, repayment timing, and program terms are determined by the provider and may change. Homeowners must review the official application, disclosures, and loan agreement before accepting any financing.
