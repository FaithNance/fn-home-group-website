GM and Manufacturing Families

Real Estate Help Built for GM and Manufacturing Families

Real estate decisions are difficult enough without trying to manage them around rotating shifts, overtime, production schedules, family responsibilities, and a possible relocation. I understand that life because I lived it.

I spent nearly a decade working for General Motors before becoming a full time Realtor. My husband still works for GM, so manufacturing life continues to be part of our family. I understand the schedule, the terminology, the pressure, and how difficult it can be to coordinate a major move while still keeping everyday life running.
