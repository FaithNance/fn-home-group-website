Our Commitment

FN Home Group is committed to making this website usable and accessible to as many visitors as possible, including people who use assistive technology. We aim to follow relevant accessibility guidelines, including the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG), as a general reference point.

Accessibility Features on This Site

  • Semantic HTML structure with a logical heading hierarchy
  • Visible keyboard focus states on interactive elements
  • Descriptive alternative text on images (including placeholder labeling)
  • Labeled form fields with clear required-field indicators
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and background
  • Support for reduced-motion preferences
  • A "skip to main content" link for keyboard and screen reader users

Ongoing Efforts

Accessibility is an ongoing process. As this website continues to grow with new pages and content, we will continue reviewing it for accessibility improvements.

Feedback

If you encounter any accessibility barriers while using this website, please let us know so we can address them. Contact Faith Nance at faithnance@epique.me or 931-332-6089.

This statement describes a general good-faith effort toward accessibility and is not a certification of full compliance with any specific legal standard. Faith recommends a professional accessibility audit for a comprehensive review.
