Fair Housing Statement

⌂ Equal Housing Opportunity

Faith Nance, FN Home Group, and Epique Realty are committed to the letter and spirit of U.S. federal fair housing laws, including the Fair Housing Act, as well as applicable Tennessee fair housing laws.

It is illegal to discriminate in the sale, rental, or financing of housing, or in the provision of real estate brokerage services, on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, or disability. Tennessee and local fair housing protections may extend to additional protected classes.

Our Commitment

Faith is committed to providing equal professional service to all clients and customers regardless of any protected characteristic. All community descriptions, neighborhood information, and general guidance on this website are intended to be neutral and factual, and are not intended to steer any client toward or away from any community, neighborhood, or area based on a protected characteristic.

Reporting Concerns

If you believe you have experienced housing discrimination, you may contact the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) or the Tennessee Human Rights Commission.

This statement is general in nature and does not constitute legal advice regarding fair housing compliance.
