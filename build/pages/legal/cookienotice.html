Cookie Notice

Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website uses Google Analytics 4, a website measurement service provided by Google, to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar browser technologies, such as browser storage, to recognize a returning browser and to measure how pages are used. Basic functionality, such as form spam protection through Netlify, may also involve limited technical data handling that is required for the site to work correctly.

Analytics Stays Off Until You Accept

No analytics cookie is loaded or stored before you accept analytics. On your first visit a short privacy notice appears with two choices: Accept Analytics and Decline Analytics. Google Consent Mode keeps analytics storage denied until you select Accept Analytics. If you select Decline Analytics, no analytics cookie is stored and no analytics event is sent.

Your choice is remembered in your own browser using browser storage, so the notice does not appear during every page visit. That saved choice is used only to remember your preference.

Changing Your Choice

You can change your analytics preference at any time by selecting Privacy Choices in the website footer. The privacy notice opens again with your current selection shown, and you can accept or decline analytics.

What Analytics Collects

When you accept analytics, general usage information is collected, such as page visits, traffic sources, device and browser information, approximate geographic area, button interactions, downloads, and form submission events. Button and link interactions are recorded using the label and destination that are written into the website itself.

What Is Not Sent To Analytics

Names, email addresses, telephone numbers, message contents, and form responses are not intentionally sent to Google Analytics. When you submit a form, analytics records only that a submission happened on that page, never the answers you typed. Telephone and email link clicks are recorded as an event and a page address only, without the number or address itself.

Advertising

This website does not use advertising tracking, personalized advertising, or advertising cookies.
