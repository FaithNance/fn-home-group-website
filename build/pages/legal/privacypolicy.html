Information We Collect

When you use forms on this website (such as the Contact form, Buyer or Seller Consultation forms, Home Value request, Relocation request, or resource download requests), we may collect information you voluntarily provide, which may include your name, email address, phone number, property address, and any additional notes or messages you submit.

How Information Is Used

Information submitted through this website is used to respond to your inquiry, provide requested information or resources, and, where you've consented, to follow up about real estate services. We do not sell your personal information.

Form Submissions & Third-Party Services

This website uses Netlify Forms to process form submissions. Submitted information is stored and transmitted through Netlify's infrastructure. Please review Netlify's privacy policy for details about how they handle data.

Cookies & Tracking

This website uses Google Analytics 4, a website measurement service provided by Google, to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar browser technologies, such as browser storage, to measure how pages are used.

When you accept analytics, general usage information is collected, such as page visits, traffic sources, device and browser information, approximate geographic area, button interactions, downloads, and form submission events. Button and link interactions are recorded using the label and destination that are written into the website itself.

Names, email addresses, telephone numbers, message contents, and form responses are not intentionally sent to Google Analytics. When you submit a form, analytics records only that a submission happened on that page, never the answers you typed. Telephone and email link clicks are recorded as an event and a page address only, without the number or address itself.

Analytics storage is denied until you choose to accept it. On your first visit a short privacy notice appears with the choices Accept Analytics and Decline Analytics, and Google Consent Mode keeps analytics storage denied until you accept. Your choice is remembered in your own browser so the notice does not appear during every page visit, and you can change it at any time by selecting Privacy Choices in the website footer. This website does not use advertising tracking or personalized advertising. The accompanying Cookie Notice describes this in more detail.

Your Choices

You may contact Faith at any time to ask what information has been collected about you, to request corrections, or to request removal from any contact list, subject to applicable law.
