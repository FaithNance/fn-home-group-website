A General Transaction Timeline

This is a general overview only — your actual contract dates and deadlines will always take priority.

MilestoneGeneral TimingNotes
Contract ExecutedDay 0Official start of your transaction timeline.
Earnest Money DueTypically within a few days of contractConfirm exact deadline in your contract.
Inspection PeriodTypically 7-14 daysVaries by contract terms.
Appraisal (if financing)Typically 2-3 weeks inOrdered by your lender.
Final Loan ApprovalShortly before closingDepends on lender processing time.
